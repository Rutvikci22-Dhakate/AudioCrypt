How It Works

AudioCrypt uses a sophisticated hybrid encryption system that combines the best of both asymmetric and symmetric cryptography.

1️⃣

Register & Get Keys

Create your account and receive a unique RSA-4096 key pair generated securely on our servers.

2️⃣

Upload & Encrypt

Upload your audio files. We generate a random AES key, encrypt your file, then encrypt the AES key with your RSA public key.

3️⃣

Secure Storage

Your encrypted files are stored securely. Only you can decrypt them using your private key.

4️⃣

Download & Decrypt

Decrypt your files instantly. The original format and quality are perfectly preserved.
