Our Technology

🔐 Encryption Standards

  • • RSA-4096: Asymmetric key encryption for maximum security
  • • AES-256-GCM: Advanced symmetric encryption with authentication
  • • Secure Random: Cryptographically secure key generation
  • • Zero Padding: Protection against timing attacks

🎵 Audio Support

  • • Format Preservation: No quality loss during encryption
  • • Multi-Format: MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, M4A support
  • • Large Files: Handle files up to 500MB efficiently
  • • Batch Processing: Encrypt multiple files simultaneously

Our Security Principles

🛡️ Defense in Depth

Multiple layers of security ensure your data remains protected even if one layer is compromised.

🔑 Client-Side Encryption

All encryption happens on your device. Your keys and data never leave your control.

🔒 Perfect Forward Secrecy

Each encryption session uses unique keys, ensuring past and future sessions remain secure.

🚫 No Data Collection

We don't collect, store, or analyze your personal data or usage patterns.
